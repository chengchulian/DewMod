BD 助手 / BD Assistant

在大厅浏览、分享和应用 Build。

• 点击可拖动图标打开助手，支持「我的／公开／全部」筛选，本人作品显示关联栏位。
• 一键应用旅行者记忆、星座与既视感到自己当前选择的栏位。应用前请取消准备，并解锁所需内容。
• QWERD 每个位置最多四个精粹；技能可留空，同种精粹仅保留一份。单击标记核心，右键清空。
• 拖动技能或精粹互换位置，保留各自核心标记。身份记忆固定，其下方精粹照常编辑。
• 候选按稀有度分类，英雄记忆再按角色排列，支持搜索与拖放。
• 上传当前配装并编辑技能构筑，分享至 Steam 创意工坊。同一英雄、模式和栏位再次上传可更新原作品。

首次上传为私密，可在自动打开的 Steam 页面公开。应用成功后自动喊话：[英雄名]一键星座 Build名称 已应用。 --Bd助手mod
更新时若 Steam 明确返回原作品不存在，会备份旧作品 ID，并自动尝试新建一次私密作品。

可选联动「转职」和「构筑需求消息」。普通模式禁用转职 Build；需求联动只更新当前英雄当前栏位。

支持游戏全部 13 种语言，切换语言时同步刷新界面。Esc 逐层关闭弹窗。使用时请保持 Steam 在线；技能和精粹仍需在局内正常获取。

Browse, share and apply builds from the lobby.

• Open the assistant with its draggable icon. Filter Mine/Public/All; your own builds show their linked upload page.
• Apply traveler memories, constellations and deja vu to your currently selected page. Cancel ready status first; required items must be unlocked.
• Plan QWERD skills with up to four gems each. A skill may stay empty, and each gem appears only once. Click to mark Core; right-click to clear.
• Drag skills or gems to swap positions while preserving Core markers. Identity memories stay fixed; their gems remain editable.
• Browse candidates by rarity and hero, search, and drag them into your build.
• Upload your loadout and skill plan to Steam Workshop. Uploading the same hero/mode/page again updates its item. New items are private; change visibility on the Steam page that opens after upload.
• If Steam reports that the original item no longer exists, its saved ID is backed up and one replacement private item is attempted.

Optional Identity Change and Build Need Messages integration. Identity builds require Identity Change mode. Supports all 13 game languages. Esc closes one popup layer. Steam must be online; skills and gems are still obtained through normal play.
