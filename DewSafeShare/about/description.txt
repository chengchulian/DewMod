[h1]DewSafeShare | 安全共享[/h1]

Keep valuable drops private until you choose to share them.

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*]Skills and essences bought from shops remain visible only to the owner until pinged.
[*]Skills and essences removed from a hero now follow the same rule.
[*]After the owner pings the item, other players can see it for a configurable duration.
[*]Default reveal duration: 10 seconds.
[*]After the reveal window ends, the item becomes private again if it is still on the ground.
[*]Host-only mod: it only takes effect when enabled by the host/server. It has no effect when you join someone else's room as a client.
[/list]

[h2]中文说明[/h2]
安全共享会让技能和精粹在被物主主动 ping 之前保持私有。

[list]
[*]从商店购买的技能和精粹：默认只有购买者可见。
[*]从英雄身上卸下或丢下的技能和精粹：现在也默认只有物主可见。
[*]物主 ping 物品后，其他玩家可以在配置时间内看到。
[*]默认公开时间为 10 秒。
[*]公开时间结束后，如果物品仍在地上，会再次隐藏。
[*]仅主机 Mod：只有房主/服务器启用时才会生效。作为客机加入别人的房间时无效。
[/list]

[h2]Configuration[/h2]
[b]Reveal duration after ping[/b]: controls how many seconds an item stays visible to other players after the owner pings it.
