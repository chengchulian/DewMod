无限视距MOD为您带来更广阔的视野体验，突破原有游戏视野限制，让您能够更清楚地观察远处景象。

主要功能：
- 扩大摄像机视野范围，可自定义缩放倍数
- 可调节缩放级别步长


你可以在此MOD目录下的i18n文件夹内添加你所在地区的本地化翻译json文件

The MoreVision MOD brings you a broader visual experience, breaking through the original game's visual limitations and allowing you to observe distant scenes more clearly.

Main functions:
-Expand the camera's field of view and customize the zoom factor
-Adjustable zoom level step size

You can add your local translation files in the 'i18n' folder under this mod directory.
