本模组可以限制攻速数值爆炸导致的多人联机时体验差,溢出的攻速可以转换为伤害加成

主要功能:
限制攻速上限
使溢出的攻速转换为伤害加成

这是一个仅服务器(房主)Mod,客户端无需安装也可以使用其功能,且客户端调整属性无效

This mod can limit the poor multiplayer experience caused by the explosion of attack speed values, and the overflow attack speed can be converted into damage bonuses

Main functions:
Limit the upper limit of attack speed
Convert the overflow attack speed into damage bonus

This is a server only (homeowner) Mod,
 and the client can use its features without installation,
  and adjusting properties on the client is invalid
